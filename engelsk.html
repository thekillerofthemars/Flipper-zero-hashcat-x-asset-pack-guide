Flipper Marauder x Hashcat

(remember to delete necessary logs and pcaps to avoid confusion. If you don't get 4 handshakes, you must start over.)

  1. 1. Open the Marauder app on your Flipper Zero and scan for "all" and wait until your network appears
  2. 2. Check the list for your network number, then select that network in "Select"
  3. 3. Go back and navigate down to "sniff", select PMKID, then choose either Targeted Passive or Active
  4. 4. Disconnect and reconnect the device at least 4 times on the selected network. Others can do this too.
  5. 5. After that, go back to AP Info. It should say Complete EAPOL: true
  6. 6. Connect the Flipper to your PC, go to SD Card/apps_data/marauder/pcaps and download the correct .pcap file
  7. 7. Open the file in Wireshark and search for "eapol" in the filter. Check if you have 4 handshakes, if you have you can proceed.
  8. 8. Upload the file to PCAP Converter to Hashcat
  9. 9. After conversion, take the converted file and place it in the root Hashcat folder. After that type "cmd" in the top directory bar and hit ENTER
  10. 10. Then type: hashcat -m 22000 "name of converted file" "name of list with passwords.txt" -w 3
  11. 11. Hit ENTER and wait for the results
  12. The bigger the wordlist, the higher the chance of cracking the password

Asset Pack for Momentum

  1. 1. Find a GIF you like and download it
  2. 2. Go to Resize GIF and resize it to Width: 128, Height: 64
  3. 3. Click "Save" after resizing
  4. 4. Then go to Split GIFs into frames and click "Download"
  5. 5. After ezgif, go to F0-AssetPack-Maker
  6. 6. On the website do the following:
    Upload the folder with GIF frames
    Pack name and Animation name: Whatever you want
    Pack type: Animations only
    Weight: 50
  7. 7. Click "Export Asset Pack"
  8. 8. After downloading, unzip the file, go into the animations_xxxxxxx folder and open "meta.txt"
  9. 9. In "meta.txt" delete the following:
    Min butthurt: 0
Max butthurt: 14
Min level: 1
Max level: 30
Weight: 1
  10. 10. Change the following: Duration 5 → 3600 and add Active cooldown: 0 then Bubble slots: 0
  11. 11. The final result should look like this: 
    Width: 128
Height: 64
Passive frames: 1
Active frames: 0
Frames order: 0
Active cycles: 0
Frame rate: 5 (This can be changed in Anim speed)
Duration: 3600
Active cooldown: 0
Bubble slots: 0
  12. 12. Copy the asset pack into SD Card/asset_packs in qFlipper
Click on any step to mark it as completed
